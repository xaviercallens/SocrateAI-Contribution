# What we want to try next

*Stated as hopes, not results. Nothing on this page has been done yet.*

The method described here (claims written down first, tests that can fail, a separate checker, the proof checker as judge, negative results published) was built on questions from mathematics and physics. We would like to find out whether it is useful on questions closer to everyday concerns. All of the following would be **computational questions only**: calculations, models and formal proofs. Not experiments, not products, not advice.

| Area | The kind of question we would like to try | What it would not be |
|---|---|---|
| **Energy** | Can parts of the calculations used for energy systems (for example the stiff equations solved in plasma, chemistry or materials models) be given checked error margins, the way the cosmic distances were? | Not a claim about any energy device, and not a design. |
| **Mathematics of cancer models** | Can published mathematical models of tumour growth or treatment scheduling have some of their stated properties checked by a proof checker, and do any of those properties turn out to be false? | **Not medical research, not a treatment, not a cure, and not advice.** It would only check mathematics already published by others. |
| **New materials** | Does the triangle-lattice work extend in any checked way to arrangements of atoms or particles of practical interest, and where does it fail? | Not a claim to have found a new material. |
| **Countermeasures to carbon emissions** | Can calculations that appear in published models of carbon capture or emissions be re-run and checked, with their assumptions stated? | **No claim of any effect on the climate.** It would check calculations, nothing more. |

## How it will be done

- **A preregistered proposal will be published here before any result.** It will state each question, what would count as success or failure, and the stopping rules. It is being written separately. Until it appears, no hypothesis is announced.
- The same rules as before will apply: levels A, L, C and X; a test that must pass and one that must fail; a checker separate from the maker; negative results published.
- Every result will say plainly that it was produced by AI agents and audited by AI agents, until a human expert has reviewed it.
- We would welcome specialists in these fields, who could tell us which questions are worth asking and which are not. Please [open an issue](../CONTRIBUTING.md).

We may well find that the method adds little in some of these areas. That would be worth knowing, and it would be reported too.
