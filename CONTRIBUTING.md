# Contributing

Thank you for looking. The most useful contribution to this repository is **a well-argued challenge**.

## Red-team issues are welcome

If you think a claim here is wrong, overstated, badly labelled or not reproducible, please open an issue. You do not need to be sure; you need to say why. A useful challenge says:

1. **Which claim** it is about: the Zenodo DOI, and the ledger row id if there is one (such as `YK-A-0001` in `elenchus/discoveries/*/ledger/ledger.json`).
2. **What you think is wrong**: a false statement, a level that is too high, a Lean statement that does not say what its description says, a calculation that does not reproduce, a missing caveat.
3. **How you checked**: what you ran (with versions), what you read and what you got.

Especially welcome:
- **statement audits**: reading a level-A Lean statement and checking whether it really says what the plain description says (no person has done this yet for any result here);
- re-runs of the Lean checks or of the calculations on your own machine;
- missing caveats in the plain-language texts (README, docs, books).

The intention is that a challenge that turns out to be right is acknowledged and its correction published, as earlier corrections were ([docs/what-we-got-wrong.md](docs/what-we-got-wrong.md)).

## Other issues

Questions, typos, broken links and translation problems (English and French) are welcome too.

## Pull requests

Small fixes to the texts (typos, links, clearer wording) are welcome as pull requests. Please do not edit:
- the ledgers or evidence files in `elenchus/discoveries/`, which are records of published work;
- the PDFs in `books/`, which are byte-for-byte copies of the Zenodo records.

Open an issue instead, and the correction can be published in the open.

By contributing, you agree that your contribution is released under the licences in [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md).

## Contact

There is no e-mail contact. Please open an issue.

## Be respectful

Disagree with claims, not with people. See [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
